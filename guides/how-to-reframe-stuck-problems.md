# How-To Guide: Reframe Intractable Problems Using P-H Pairs

> **Diátaxis How-To Guide:** Step-by-step instructions for breaking functional fixedness, importing cross-domain coordinate systems, and structuring open crowdsourced challenges (Prof. Karim Lakhani's methodology).

---

## Prerequisites & Goal

- **Prerequisites:** An engineering, scientific, or commercialization roadblock where incremental optimization has stalled for $>2$ weeks.
- **Goal:** Transform an intractable problem representation into an orthogonal coordinate system that reveals immediate solution pathways.

---

## Step 1: Diagnose the Representational Trap

Review your team's current behavior against the **Trap Checklist**:
- [ ] Team members are logging overtime to make an algorithm or chemical process 3% faster.
- [ ] Increasing compute or capital is the only proposed solution.
- [ ] Disagreements revolve around fine-tuning parameters within a single discipline.

> [!IMPORTANT] The Simon Principle
> If you are computing mental arithmetic combinations to sum to 15, do not compute faster. **Map the 3×3 Magic Square.**

---

## Step 2: Identify the Underlying Mathematical or Physical Structure

Isolate the abstract mathematical or physical dynamic underlying the roadblock:
1. Is it a **Combinatorial Explosion**? (Too many possibilities to compute linearly).
2. Is it an **Oscillation / Instability** problem? (Feedback loops out of phase).
3. Is it a **Boundary Layer / Surface Resistance** problem? (Diffusion limits).
4. Is it an **Incentive Alignment / Game-Theoretic Deadlock**?

---

## Step 3: Map Cross-Domain Analogies

Ask: *"Which distant scientific or industrial disciplines solve this exact mathematical dynamic on a daily basis?"*

| If Your Roadblock Resembles... | Look Into This Distant Discipline... | For This Established Mental Model / Heuristic |
|---|---|---|
| Unpredictable erratic pricing fluctuations | Classical Thermodynamics & Physics | Brownian motion & heat diffusion equations |
| Neurological seizure spikes | Electrical Circuit Engineering | Damped oscillator filters & circuit breakers |
| Microscopic particle clustering | Acoustic Vibration / Fluid Mechanics | Ultrasonic standing wave de-agglomeration |
| Intractable multi-protein folding | Metallurgy & Polymer Chemistry | Polymer crystallization kinetics |

---

## Step 4: Draft a Crowdsourced Challenge Brief (LISH Syntax)

If internal cross-domain exploration fails, broadcast the challenge to an open solver network (InnoCentive, Kaggle, Topcoder):

1. **Remove All Proprietary Context:** Strip company names, brand names, and end-product references.
2. **Define the Abstract Function:**
   - *Bad:* *"How can Colgate prevent fine fluoride dust from blowing away in our New Jersey packaging plant?"*
   - *Good:* *"Seeking methods to impart a localized temporary electrostatic charge to dry submicron particulates traveling through high-velocity air nozzles."*
3. **Specify Objective Evaluation Criteria:** State exact numerical metrics (e.g., must settle 99% of dust in $<2$ seconds without altering chemical pH).
4. **Offer Tiered Solvers Incentives:** Establish a prize structure rewarding novel P-H pairs rather than incremental improvements.

---

## Expected Result

You now have an orthogonal coordinate representation of the problem, eliminating functional fixedness and unlocking trivial solutions that previously appeared computationally impossible.
