## Stage-by-Stage Audit Rubric

*Scoring: 0 = Not Addressed; 1 = Inadequate / High Risk; 2 = Acceptable / Partially Validated; 3 = Robust / Independently Verified.*

### Stage 1: Scientific Discovery & The Seed
- [ ] **1.1 Phenomenon Isolation:** Fundamental physical mechanism isolated from laboratory setup artifacts. *(Score: 0–3)*
- [ ] **1.2 Repeatability:** Effect reliably reproduced across variable ambient temperatures/pressures. *(Score: 0–3)*
- [ ] **1.3 Broad Provisional IP:** Patent claims drafted broadly without constraining application verticals. *(Score: 0–3)*

- [ ] **3.1 Universal Functional Analysis:** Technology deconstructed into active S-A-O triads. *(Score: 0–3)*
- [ ] **3.2 Customer Activity Chain:** End-to-end friction mapped across at least 2 candidate verticals. *(Score: 0–3)*
- [ ] **3.3 Quantified MPVs:** Identified 1–2 decisive parameters offering an order-of-magnitude (10x) leap. *(Score: 0–3)*

- [ ] **4.3 Architectural Alignment:** Unit economics confirm operational delivery cost allows >70% contribution margin. *(Score: 0–3)*

### Evaluation Scoring Scale:
- **52 – 63 Points:** **A-Tier / Investment-Grade Venture.** Proceed to institutional scaling.
- **38 – 51 Points:** **B-Tier / Promising with Structural Gaps.** Remediate specific stage deficiencies before committing capital.
- **< 38 Points:** **High Mortality Risk.** Venture has jumped stages prematurely; return to earlier stage gates.
