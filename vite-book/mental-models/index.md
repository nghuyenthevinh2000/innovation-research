# Mental Models & Cognitive Architecture

> **The Foundational Lenses, Decision Paradigms, and Heuristic Frameworks of Technology Commercialization**

---

## Overview

A mental model is a compressed representation of how a system behaves. In complex deep-tech entrepreneurship—where technical uncertainty, market antibodies, and capital constraints collide—relying on standard business intuition almost always leads to disaster.

This directory provides the intellectual toolkit to navigate the innovation landscape systematically.

## Core Mental Model Categories

1. **Cognitive Search & Emergence:**
   - Biological Evolution of Breakthroughs ($\text{Variation} + \text{Selection} + \text{Iteration}$)
   - Perspective-Heuristic Landscape Search ($\text{Difficulty} = f(P, H)$)
   - Magic Square Coordinate Shift (Transforming intractable arithmetic into visual Tic-Tac-Toe)
   - Scott Page Cognitive Diversity Theorem (*Diversity Trumps Ability*)

2. **Technology Translation & Needs Discovery:**
   - Universal Functional Thinking (*Function is the goal; technology is merely a means*)
   - Miles Value Ratio & Main Parameters of Value (MPV)
   - Jobs-to-Be-Done & Outcomes-Driven Innovation (ODI)
   - Linkages-Driven Customer Activity Chains

3. **Venture Architecture & Market Dynamics:**
   - Business Architecture Symbiosis ($\text{Business Model} + \text{Operating Model}$)
   - Operating Model Triad ($\text{Structure} + \text{Capabilities} + \text{Assets}$)
   - The 10x Imperative & Market Antibodies
   - Contact Sport Commercialization vs. Linear Relay Race

4. **Entrepreneurial Finance & De-Risking:**
   - The Firm as a Cash Engine ($\text{Cash} \rightarrow \text{Assets} \rightarrow \text{Cash} + \Delta\text{Cash}$)
   - The Lemonade Stand Working Capital Model
   - Discriminating Experiments & Hypothesis Staging
   - Debt vs Equity Risk-Return Asymmetry
   - The VC Power Law & 10-Year Fund Horizon
