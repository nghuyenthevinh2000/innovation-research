# How-To Guide: Deconstruct Technology Seeds into Universal Functions

> **Diátaxis How-To Guide:** Step-by-step instructions for breaking psychological inertia, decomposing proprietary technology into universal functions, and discovering unexpected cross-industry market applications (Dr. Sam Kogan's Gen5 methodology).

---

## Prerequisites & Goal

- **Prerequisites:** A documented laboratory discovery or engineering capability (TRL 2–4).
- **Goal:** Produce a validated **Function-Application Matrix** mapping your proprietary seed across at least 3 unrelated global industries in under 2 hours.

---

## Step 1: Strip Proprietary Jargon & Domain Nouns

1. Write out the current description of your technology in 1–2 paragraphs.
2. Circle all domain-specific nouns, proprietary material names, and industry jargon (e.g., *"nanoporous graphene oxide"*, *"hydrocarbon gas stream"*, *"desalination"*, *"oncology CT"*).
3. Replace all circled terms with generic physical terms:
   - *"Graphene membrane"* → *Porous physical barrier*
   - *"Hydrocarbon gas"* → *Multi-component fluid stream*
   - *"CT volumetric scan"* → *3D visual array*

---

## Step 2: Formulate Subject-Action-Object (S-A-O) Triads

Express what the technology *does* as active verbs acting upon physical targets.

| Syntax Rule | Example (Bad / Domain-Bound) | Example (Good / Universal S-A-O) |
|---|---|---|
| **Active Verb + Physical Object** | *"Purifies municipal drinking water"* | **[Membrane]** + **Separates** + **[Molecules by Size]** |
| **Active Verb + Energy / Flow** | *"Conducts fuel cell electricity"* | **[Layer]** + **Directs** + **[Ion Flow]** |
| **Active Verb + Information** | *"Diagnoses glioblastoma in brain scans"* | **[Model]** + **Isolates** + **[Visual Discrepancies]** |

---

## Step 3: Identify the Main Parameter of Value (MPV)

Define the physical ratio of utility to cost:
$$\text{MPV} = \frac{\text{Primary Performance Metric (e.g., Liters/sec, Accuracy, Megawatts)}}{\text{Total Cost of Ownership (e.g., CapEx, Power consumption, Maintenance)}}$$

Identify the 1 non-negotiable metric where your seed delivers an order-of-magnitude (10x) advantage.

---

## Step 4: Execute Cross-Industry Patent Database Searches

1. Open Google Patents, Lens.org, or USPTO database.
2. Query using your active S-A-O verbs and objects:
   ```text
   ("separates fluid" OR "removes dissolved solids") AND ("ambient temperature" OR "mechanical separation")
   ```
3. Filter out your native academic domain (e.g., exclude "desalination" or "water treatment").
4. Inspect patent classifications in adjacent industries:
   - Class B01D: Separation of liquids and gases.
   - Class A23L: Food and beverage processing.
   - Class H01L: Semiconductor chemical washing.

---

## Step 5: Construct the Function-Application Matrix

Compile your findings into a decision table:

| Universal Function | Adjacent Industry Vertical | Current Industry Workaround | Advantage of Your Seed (MPV) |
|---|---|---|---|
| *Separates fluid at ambient temp* | Pulp & Paper Processing | High-energy thermal boiling (\$2.5B cost) | 90% reduction in thermal energy consumption |
| *Separates fluid at ambient temp* | Dairy Whey Protein Concentration | Reverse osmosis filters that foul easily | 5x longer filter lifespan without clogging |
| *Separates fluid at ambient temp* | Biofuel Algae Dewatering | Centrifugal mechanical drying | 80% lower operational electrical draw |
